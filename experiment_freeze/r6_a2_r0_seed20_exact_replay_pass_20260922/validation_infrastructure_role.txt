They are NOT production release code.

- historical hash_backbone namespace;
- R6 internal model-hash namespace;
- exact post-B model identity;
- final-refresh immutability;
- exact frozen scientific outputs.

All original wrapped functions were invoked exactly once and their outputs were
returned unchanged.

MvCAN is a composite object, not torch.nn.Module. Its ordered scientific state
lives in model.autoencoders[view_id], each of which is an nn.Module.

Capture: save an ordered tuple of non-empty dictionaries, one per view, mapping
each autoencoder state_dict parameter/buffer name to detach().cpu().clone().
View namespaces and order 0..V-1 are retained.

Restore: deepcopy the composite, require exact view count, then for each view
call autoencoder.cpu(), autoencoder.load_state_dict(per_view_state, strict=True),
and autoencoder.train(). Require every parameter/buffer on CPU and training=True.

Identity gate: model_state_snapshot(restored) must match H0 exactly at aggregate
and per-view model_hash, encoder_parameter_hash, decoder_parameter_hash, and
cluster_centers_hash. Otherwise RESTORED_H0_MODEL_NOT_EXACT fails closed and T
is not allowed.

H/F/W/T scientific definitions and carrier decision rules are unchanged.

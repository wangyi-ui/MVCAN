F_PROVENANCE remains reconstruct_current_msrc(device) and is used only for
validate_reconstruction_against_manifest(). It is not compared directly to H.

F_ARM is rebuilt from F_PROVENANCE q_local/q_aligned/M_v by the same
_carrier_from_aligned(..., device="cuda:0") used for H, W, and T. All four
diagnostic arms therefore share CUDA R2/R3 numerical semantics; H/F/W/T carrier
comparisons use H_ARM/F_ARM/W_ARM/T_ARM only.

After the expensive historical replay returns, a sibling
<output-dir>.partial.json is atomically written using a temp file plus
os.replace. It records H0-H4 hashes, observed/expected H hashes, current
provenance state, completed arms, device, failure class, and full_gt_loaded=false.
The formal output directory is never created on a failure path.

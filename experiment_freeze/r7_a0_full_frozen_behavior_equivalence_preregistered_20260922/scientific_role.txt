R7 SCIENTIFIC ROLE

R7 is the final validation stage of the clean-code extraction route.

R7 introduces no new scientific mechanism and owns no scientific transformation.
It validates that the independently extracted R1-R6 components compose into a
dependency-closed, ownership-closed, scientifically frozen, behaviorally
equivalent clean release within the preregistered evidence scope.

Frozen semantic contract:

- Sparse labels determine semantic direction/content.
- Frozen U_cycle determines action validity.
- Information Utility is unchanged.

Information Utility is not:
- generic feature importance;
- pseudo-label confidence;
- a continuous posterior weight;
- memory-write confidence;
- pseudo-anchor priority.

R7 must not change or introduce Information Utility, U_cycle recalibration,
U_tilde, feature/fusion gating, pseudo-label supervision, memory, losses,
optimization objectives, phase order, target refresh, seed policy,
hyperparameters, or dataset evidence scope.

R7 FROZEN-BEHAVIOR EQUIVALENCE DEFINITION

Let:

F_hist(X, C, xi)

denote historical frozen behavior, and let:

F_rel(X, C, xi)

denote extracted release behavior.

R7 does not require source-code textual equality.

R7 requires equality of the preregistered scientific observables:

Pi_Omega(F_rel) = Pi_Omega(F_hist)

within the frozen evidence scope defined by scientific_observables_contract.txt
and evidence_boundary.txt.

Full behavior must not be reduced to metrics-only parity. Metrics equality alone
is insufficient.

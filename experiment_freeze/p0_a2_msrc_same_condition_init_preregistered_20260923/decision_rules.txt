P0-A2 PREREGISTERED DIAGNOSTIC RULES

No absolute ACC/NMI/ARI threshold is used.

For each of ACC, NMI, and ARI record:
DeltaRelation = UNIFORM - BASE
DeltaUtility = TRUE_U - UNIFORM
DeltaFull = TRUE_U - BASE

The diagnostic case is selected from ACC ordering only:

Case A: TRUE_U > UNIFORM > BASE
Interpretation: same-condition initialization restores positive relation and
utility contributions. Prefer initialization mismatch as the explanation for
P0-A1; do not modify R2-R5.

Case B: UNIFORM > BASE and TRUE_U <= UNIFORM
Interpretation: relation transfer is positive while action-specific U still
requires an R2 diagnosis on MSRC.

Case C: all remaining orderings, including BASE >= UNIFORM and/or the absence
of the Case-A/Case-B pattern.
Interpretation: the semantic relation path does not establish a stable positive
gain under the same-condition initialization; only then proceed to layered
R3/R4/R2 diagnosis.

No case directly authorizes a method or hyperparameter change.

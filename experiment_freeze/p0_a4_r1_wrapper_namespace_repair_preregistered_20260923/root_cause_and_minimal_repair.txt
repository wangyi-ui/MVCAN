ROOT CAUSE

msrc_native_stagewise_parity.py incorrectly read TRAINING_SEED from
experiments.paper.diagnostics.p0_a3_protocol, where that symbol does not
exist. The correct frozen owner is
current_initializer.protocol (msrc_p0_a2_protocol), whose TRAINING_SEED is 20.

CLASSIFICATION

DIAGNOSTIC_WRAPPER_PROTOCOL_NAMESPACE_BUG

This is not a scientific divergence, initializer failure, RNG divergence, or
method failure.

MINIMAL REPAIR

1. Remove the now-unused p0_a3_protocol import.
2. Read current_seed from current_initializer.protocol.TRAINING_SEED.
3. Read historical_seed from
   legacy.MSRC_RUNTIME_SPEC.expected_config["training"]["seed"].
4. Fail closed unless current_seed == 20, historical_seed == 20, and the two
   seeds are equal.
5. Pass current_seed to historical prepare_native_backbone.
6. Populate report.training_seed from current_seed and record the validated
   historical_training_seed separately.

No seed is inferred, rewritten, or automatically corrected.
